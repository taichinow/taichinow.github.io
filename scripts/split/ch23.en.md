## Chapter 23: The 30-minute daily form: structure, focus, and recovery

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: The 30-minute daily form is *the* workhorse practice. It includes everything: standing, form, and recovery. For most 3.0 students, this is the practice that *transforms* the body. After 6 months of daily 30-minute practice, students report visible changes in posture, balance, and calm. |
| **The 30-minute structure** (Cấu trúc 30 phút): |
| **Minutes 0-5: Warm-up Standing (Trụ Thế Khởi động)**: Bì Bộ or Ôm Thụ. Settle, soften, breathe. |
| **Minutes 5-10: Joint Loosening (Khớp mềm)**: Slow, gentle rotations of all major joints: ankles, knees, hips, spine, shoulders, elbows, wrists, neck. 30 seconds each. |
| **Minutes 10-35: The Form (Hình Thức)**: Do the 24-Form (or partial form) at your slow pace. If you've only learned 6 forms, do those 6 forms 3 times. |
| **Minutes 35-40: Standing Recovery (Trụ Thế Phục hồi)**: Bì Bộ. Soften. Breathe. 5 minutes. |
| **Minutes 40-45: Floor cool-down (Hạ nhiệt sàn)**: Sit or lie. Body scan from feet to head. Notice any residual tension. |
| **Minutes 45-50: Closing ritual (Nghi lễ đóng)**: One final Khởi Thức or simple bow. Mark the practice as complete. |
| **Total: 50 minutes** (not 30 — adjust to 30 if needed): If 50 minutes is too long, here's the *minimum effective dose*: 5 min standing + 20 min form + 5 min standing = 30 minutes. |
