## Chapter 26: The 20-minute home practice (no teacher needed)

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: After 6-12 months of class practice, you should be able to do a complete 20-minute practice at home *without a teacher*. This is the *graduation* of 3.0. The 20-minute practice is what you'll do for the next 30 years. |
| **The 20-Minute Home Protocol**: |
| **Minutes 0-5: Stand** (5 phút trụ): Ôm Thụ or Bì Bộ. Settle, feel weight, soften, breathe into Dantian. |
| **Minutes 5-15: Form** (10 phút hình): Do the 24-Form (or what you know). Slow, breathing with each movement. If you know all 24 forms, do them in order. If you only know 6, do those 6 twice. |
| **Minutes 15-20: Stand + Close** (5 phút trụ + đóng): Bì Bộ. Soften, breathe, end with Khởi Thức to close the practice. |
| **The "no teacher" requirements**: |
| 1. You can do Khởi Thức without looking at notes. |
| 2. You can do 6+ forms without notes. |
| 3. You remember the 5-point adjustment. |
| 4. You can breathe into the Dantian. |
| 5. You can name the 9 points of relaxation. |
| **If you can't do these 5 things, you're not ready for solo practice.** |
| **The 50+ liberation**: For 50+ people, the ability to do a 20-minute home practice is *liberation*. It means: no gym membership needed, no class schedule to keep, no weather to fight, no transportation. Just you, 20 minutes, and the practice that will keep you healthy for the next 30 years. The 20-minute practice is the "vaccination" against age-related decline. |
