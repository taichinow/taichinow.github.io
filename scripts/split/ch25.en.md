## Chapter 25: The 3 conditions Taichi heals: balance, blood pressure, sleep

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: Of all the health benefits of Taichi, three stand out as the *most evidence-supported* by Western medical research: (1) Balance, (2) Blood Pressure, (3) Sleep. Each is a major 50+ concern, and each has been shown to improve measurably with consistent Taichi practice. |
| **1. Balance** (Thăng bằng): Falls are the #1 cause of injury-related death in 50+. Taichi reduces fall risk by 25-50% in randomized trials. Why? Because the slow, deliberate weight-shifting in the form is *exactly* what the vestibular system needs to stay calibrated. The Cung Bộ stance strengthens the leg muscles used to recover from stumbles. The single-leg postures (Bạch Hạc Lượng Xích, Thủ Cầm Đàn) directly train the proprioceptive system. |
| **2. Blood Pressure** (Huyết áp): Hypertension affects 60-70% of 50+ people. Taichi reduces systolic BP by 5-15 mmHg in studies — *as much as a single blood pressure medication*. The mechanism: (a) lower breathing activates parasympathetic (Vagus nerve), (b) slow movement + meditation reduces cortisol, (c) consistent practice improves endothelial function (artery flexibility). |
| **3. Sleep** (Giấc ngủ): 50% of 50+ have chronic sleep issues. Taichi improves sleep quality, reduces time to fall asleep, and reduces night wakings. Why? Because (a) lower breathing calms the nervous system, (b) the slow movement + standing = physical fatigue that promotes sleep, (c) the meditation aspect quiets mental chatter. After 3 months of daily practice, most 50+ students report "I haven't slept this well in years." |
| **The "Triad" practice**: For the 50+ student whose primary concerns are (1) balance, (2) BP, (3) sleep, the most efficient practice is: 10 min of *standing balance* work (Cung Bộ, single-leg holds), 10 min of *breath and meditation* (Ôm Thụ with Dantian focus), 10 min of *slow form* (whatever you know). This 30-minute protocol specifically targets the Triad. |
