## Chapter 27: The 5-minute "Reset" practice for busy days

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: There will be days when you "don't have time" for the 20-minute practice. The 5-minute Reset is the *minimum effective dose* for those days. It's better than skipping. 5 minutes of Taichi beats 0 minutes of Taichi — and the 5-minute Reset can be done in a hotel room, a bathroom, an office, or a parking lot. |
| **The 5-Minute Reset Protocol**: |
| **Minute 1: 5-Point Reset** (Phút 1: 5-Điểm Reset): Stand in Bì Bộ. Run through the 5-point adjustment. Notice where you are right now. |
| **Minute 2: 9-Point Soften** (Phút 2: Mềm 9-Điểm): Top to bottom, soften the 9 points. Spend 5-7 seconds on each. |
| **Minute 3: 6 Belly Breaths** (Phút 3: 6 Thở Bụng): 6 deep belly breaths, into the Dantian. |
| **Minute 4: One Form** (Phút 4: Một Thức): Do one form you know well, slowly. Just one. |
| **Minute 5: Stand + Close** (Phút 5: Trụ + Đóng): Bì Bộ. 3 belly breaths. Khởi Thức to close. |
| **The "no excuse" rule**: |
| "I don't have time" → Reset 5 minutes. |
| "I'm traveling" → Reset 5 minutes in a hotel room. |
| "I'm at work" → Reset 5 minutes in a bathroom stall. |
| "I'm sick" → Reset 5 minutes, slowly, breathing. |
| "I forgot" → Reset 5 minutes as soon as you remember. |
| **There is no day you cannot do 5 minutes of Taichi.** |
