## Chapter 29: The 7 Deadly Sins of the 3.0 Taichi student

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: Most 3.0 students make the *same* 7 mistakes. If you can avoid these 7, you'll progress faster than 90% of beginners. The list comes from observing 30+ years of 3.0 students at the park, in classes, and in my own early years. |
| **The 7 Deadly Sins** (7 Tội Chết Người): |
| **1. Speed Demon** (Ma Tốc Độ): Trying to learn the form fast. "I want to know all 24 forms in 3 months." Result: no depth, injuries, abandonment. *Fix*: One form a week. |
| **2. Shoulder Tosser** (Vai Ném): Throwing the arms around, using muscular force. "I'll just wave my arms." Result: no Peng, no structure, no benefit. *Fix*: Sōng the shoulders first. |
| **3. Breath Holder** (Người Nín Thở): Holding the breath through the form. "I'll just move and forget the breath." Result: stress, fatigue, headaches. *Fix*: 4-7-8 breath daily. |
| **4. Knee Breaker** (Người Gãy Gối): Locking the knees in standing or pushing past the toes. "I'll straighten up like in the army." Result: knee pain, joint damage. *Fix*: Always soft knees. |
| **5. Skipping Stand** (Bỏ Qua Trụ Thế): Skipping standing meditation because "nothing is happening." "I'll just do the form, not the boring standing." Result: foundation never builds. *Fix*: 5-min stand every day. |
| **6. Form Collector** (Nhà Sưu Tầm Hình Thức): Collecting forms instead of deepening one. "I'll learn the 42-form, then the 48-form, then the 88-form." Result: surface knowledge, no internal work. *Fix*: 1 form for 1 year. |
| **7. Stop-After-Six-Months** (Dừng Sau 6 Tháng): Stopping after 6 months because "I know it now." "I've got the form down." Result: lost the deep benefits, never progresses. *Fix*: Year 1 is just the surface. Year 5+ is where the magic lives. |
| **The "Deadly Sin" test**: Read the 7. Which ones are YOU doing? Pick ONE to fix for the next 90 days. Don't try to fix all 7. Pick the worst. |
