## Chapter 14: Dan Tian breathing: lower abdomen, lower Dantian

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: Dan Tian (Đan Điền / 丹田) means "elixir field" — the body's energy center. The Lower Dantian (Hạ Đan Điền / 下丹田) is located about 2-3 inches below the navel, deep in the body's center of mass. Breathing *into* the Lower Dantian (not the chest) is the foundational breath of Taichi. Most 50+ people have never breathed this way in their adult lives. The first 6 months of Taichi is often just learning to breathe here. |
| **Why "lower" breathing matters**: The default modern breath is *upper chest* — short, fast, anxiety-driven. This is the breath of "fight or flight." Taichi uses the *lower belly* — long, slow, deep. This is the breath of "rest and digest." The physiological difference: lower breathing activates the *parasympathetic* nervous system (Vagus nerve, 10th cranial nerve), slowing heart rate, lowering blood pressure, calming the mind. The result: within 2-3 minutes of lower breathing, the body is measurably more relaxed. |
| **The 3.0 practice of Dantian breathing**: |
| **1. The Hand Check** (Kiểm tra tay): Sit or stand. Place one hand on chest, one on belly, just below the navel. As you breathe, only the *belly hand* should move. The chest hand stays still. If the chest hand is moving, you're chest-breathing. |
| **2. The 4-7-8 Breath** (Hơi thở 4-7-8): Inhale through nose for 4 counts (belly expands). Hold for 7 counts. Exhale through mouth for 8 counts. This is the "Dr. Weil" breath, a Western adaptation of Chinese breath training. After 4 cycles, you will be in a measurable relaxation state. |
| **3. The "Egg in the Belly"** (Trứng trong bụng): Imagine there's a small egg just below your navel, 2-3 inches deep. As you inhale, the egg grows. As you exhale, the egg shrinks but never disappears. This visualization makes the breath tangible. |
| **The "natural breath" transition** (Hơi thở tự nhiên): After 3-6 months of practice, the lower belly breath becomes *natural* — you don't think about it anymore. The chest breath becomes the "anomaly" you notice when stressed. This shift is one of the most significant changes Taichi brings to the 50+ body. |
| **Why 50+ needs lower breathing**: After 50, the diaphragm (the main breathing muscle) becomes weaker. The body compensates by using the upper chest and shoulders — which causes the shoulders to rise, the upper back to tighten, and anxiety to increase. Lower breathing *re-engages* the diaphragm, taking pressure off the shoulders, reducing anxiety, and improving oxygen exchange. Many 50+ people report that after 3 months of lower breathing, their sleep improves, their blood pressure drops, and their "general anxiety" is reduced. |
