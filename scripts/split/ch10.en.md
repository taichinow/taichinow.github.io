## Chapter 10: The 24-Form at a glance

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: The 24-Form (Nhị Thập Tứ Thức / 二十四式) is the *simplified* Tai Chi form, compiled in 1956 by the Chinese National Sports Committee to make Taichi accessible to the general public. It is *not* a watered-down version — it is a 4-6 minute sequence that contains the essence of the longer traditional forms (Yang style 108, Chen style 74, etc.). For a 3.0 beginner, the 24-Form is the perfect starting point. |
| **The 6 sections, 12 main movements** (6 phần, 12 động tác chính): |
| **Section 1 — Commencement (Khởi Thức)**: (1) Commencement Form (Khởi Thức) — opening, gathering. (2) Parting the Wild Horse's Mane (Dã Mã Phân Tung) — first Ward Off + Press. |
| **Section 2 — The Foundation (Nền tảng)**: (3) White Crane Spreads Its Wings (Bạch Hạc Lượng Xích) — balance, single-leg stance. (4) Brush Knee and Twist Step (Lau Tất Triền Châu) — Ward Off + Press with stepping. |
| **Section 3 — The Middle (Phần giữa)**: (5) Hand Strums the Lute (Thủ Cầm Đàn) — pushing energy, single-leg. (6) Repulse the Monkey (Đẩy Khỉ) — stepping back, redirecting. (7) Grasp the Sparrow's Tail (Cầm Quyết Thức — Trầm Thủ Lạc Chưởng) — the master sequence: Ward Off → Rollback → Press → Push. |
| **Section 4 — The Second Half (Phần sau)**: (8) Fair Lady Weaves at the Shuttle (Thương Nữ Thác Cơ) — diagonal stepping. (9) Cloud Hands (Vân Thủ) — lateral weight shift. (10) Single Whip (Độc Lập) — full extension, the "signature" Taichi posture. |
| **Section 5 — The Closing (Phần cuối)**: (11) High Pat on Horse (Cao Đầu Mã) — knee lift, foot kick preparation. (12) Kick with Heel (Tả Hà Đạp) — the only kick. (13) Strike Tiger Left/Right (Song Hổ Đương Đầu) — fist strike. (14) Cross Hands and Closing Form (Thập Thủ Thâu Thức) — closing. |
| **The 6 sections, 12 main movements" expanded with 2nd kicks and strikes (total 24 forms)**: |
| Section 6: (15-24) Right/Left forms of "Kicking," "Strike Tiger," "Cross Hands." |
| **Why 12 main + 12 mirror = 24**: The 24-Form was designed so that most movements are repeated on both sides (left/right). This symmetry is essential for balanced development. A 3.0 student should expect to spend *much* more time on each side than the "do both sides quickly" approach used in demonstrations. |
| **The 3.0 form practice method**: |
| **Phase 1 (Months 1-3)**: One form at a time. Learn the *shape* (outside form). Don't worry about internal mechanics. |
| **Phase 2 (Months 4-6)**: Same forms, slower, with breath. Begin to feel the energy. |
| **Phase 3 (Months 7-12)**: Connect the forms. Begin to see the form as one continuous movement, not 24 separate ones. |
| **Phase 4 (Year 2+)**: Begin to find the internal mechanics (Hư/Thực, Kình/Jin, etc.). |
