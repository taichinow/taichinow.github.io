## Chapter 20: The "Hugging the Tree" posture (Ôm Thụ)

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: Zhan Zhuang (Trụ Thế / 站樁) literally means "standing post" or "standing like a stake/pole." The most common 3.0 form is *Ôm Thụ* (Hugging the Tree): stand as if you are hugging a large tree in front of you. Your arms form a circle, the body is upright, the knees are slightly bent. This looks "easy." It is the *hardest* practice in Taichi. |
| **The 5 classical standing postures** (5 tư thế trụ cổ điển): |
| **1. Vô Cực Trụ (Wuji Post)**: Stand naturally, arms at sides, 5-point adjustment. Beginner posture. |
| **2. Bì Bộ Trụ (Parallel Stance Post)**: Same as above, but feet hip-width. The 3.0 foundation. |
| **3. Ôm Thụ (Hugging the Tree)**: Arms forward, rounded, as if hugging a large tree. Eyes can be open or closed. The "signature" standing posture. |
| **4. Cầm Bố (Holding the Ball)**: One hand up, one hand down, as if holding a large ball between them. The "Peng" standing posture. |
| **5. Dị Bộ Trụ (T-Stance Post)**: One foot forward in T-stance. Advanced. |
| **The 3.0 protocol for Ôm Thụ**: |
| **Setup**: Feet hip-width (Bì Bộ), knees slightly bent (not locked), arms forward and rounded (as if holding a beach ball at chest height, but a *larger* ball — the size of a small tree), shoulders down, elbows slightly lower than shoulders, palms facing the body, fingers softly curved, eyes soft. |
| **Phase 1 — Settle (1-2 min)**: Just stand. Notice the body. Don't try to "do" anything. |
| **Phase 2 — Feel the weight (2-3 min)**: Feel the weight sinking through the legs into the floor. Notice the 4 points of the foot. |
| **Phase 3 — Soften the 9 points (2-3 min)**: Jaw, tongue, shoulders, elbows, wrists, fingers, chest, lower back, knees. |
| **Phase 4 — Breathe into Dantian (remaining time)**: Belly breathing, into the Lower Dantian. |
| **Total time**: Start with 5 minutes. After 3 months, 10 minutes. After 1 year, 20 minutes. After 3 years, 30-45 minutes. |

> *"Ôm cây. Là cây."*
