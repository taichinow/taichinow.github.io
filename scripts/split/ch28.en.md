## Chapter 28: The 3-Anchor Check, 3-Question Diagnostic, One-Word Cues

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: This chapter is the *cheat sheet* you keep in your pocket (or taped to your bathroom mirror). The whole guide, compressed into 3 systems you can use daily. |
| **The 3-Anchor Check** (3-Mỏ Neo Kiểm Tra) — before any practice: |
| **Anchor 1 — Feet** (Mỏ neo 1 — Chân): Feel the 4 points. Big toe mound, little toe mound, inside heel, outside heel. |
| **Anchor 2 — Breath** (Mỏ neo 2 — Hơi thở): One belly breath. Inhale expand, exhale contract. |
| **Anchor 3 — Crown** (Mỏ neo 3 — Đỉnh đầu): Imagine the string pulling up. Lengthen. |
| **The 3-Question Diagnostic** (3-Câu Hỏi Chẩn Đoán) — when something feels off: |
| **Q1: Am I in Peng?** (Câu 1: Tôi có trong Phòng không?): Check: am I "full and soft" or "empty and floppy" or "locked and stiff"? |
| **Q2: Am I in Hư/Thực?** (Câu 2: Tôi có trong Hư/Thực không?): Check: is one leg full, one leg empty? Is one hand active, one hand resting? |
| **Q3: Am I breathing?** (Câu 3: Tôi có đang thở không?): Check: is the breath in the belly, or in the chest? Am I holding the breath? |
| **The 8 One-Word Cues** (8 Câu Nhắc Một-Từ) — your daily vocabulary: |
| 1. **Sōng** (松) — Relax what's not needed. |
| 2. **Hư** (虛) — Be empty, ready to receive. |
| 3. **Thực** (實) — Be full, ready to express. |
| 4. **Peng** (棚) — Sphere of organized readiness. |
| 5. **Lu** (捋) — Yield, listen, redirect. |
| 6. **An** (按) — Plant the seed. |
| 7. **Ý** (意) — Intention leads the body. |
| 8. **Hưu** (休) — Rest in the stillness. |
| **The "tape to the mirror" version**: Print this page. Tape it to your bathroom mirror. Read it once each morning. The whole 3-system cheat sheet takes 30 seconds to review. |
