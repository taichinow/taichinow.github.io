## Chapter 21: The 5-minute Standing practice that changes everything

| 🇺🇸 English |
|-------------|----------------|
| **The big idea**: If you do ONLY this practice for the next 90 days, every other aspect of Taichi will improve. The 5-minute standing practice is *the most efficient Taichi practice* in existence. It touches all 4 layers (Foundation, Hư/Thực, Breath, Mind) in 5 minutes. |
| **The protocol** (5 phút mỗi ngày, 90 ngày): |
| **Minute 0-1 (Settle)**: Stand in Bì Bộ. Hands at sides. 5-point adjustment. Just be here. |
| **Minute 1-2 (Hư/Thực)**: Slowly shift weight to right leg, pause 5 seconds, shift to left, pause 5 seconds. Continue for 1 minute. Notice the Hư/Thực in each leg. |
| **Minute 2-3 (Sōng scan)**: Stay in Bì Bộ. Soften the 9 points. Top to bottom. |
| **Minute 3-4 (Breath)**: Belly breath. 6-8 breaths. Inhale expand, exhale contract. |
| **Minute 4-5 (Mind)**: Just stand. Count 5 breaths. Notice the inner critic. Return to the body. |
| **The 90-day promise**: After 90 days of 5-minute daily practice: (1) Your shoulders will be visibly lower. (2) Your breathing will be deeper without effort. (3) Your "general anxiety" will be reduced 20-40%. (4) Your balance will measurably improve. (5) You will *want* to extend to 10 minutes. |
| **The 3.0 "no time" excuse**: Most 50+ people say "I don't have time." The 5-minute practice is the *answer* to that excuse. 5 minutes is 0.35% of a day. You spend more time waiting in line at the grocery store. If you can't find 5 minutes, the issue isn't time — it's priority. The 5-minute standing practice is *the* non-negotiable Taichi commitment. |
