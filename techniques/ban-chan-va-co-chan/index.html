Foot & Ankle — The Root

Region 8: foot and ankle. Where force meets the ground; without it the other seven regions are decoration.

Body MechanicsHead & NeckSpineThoraxShoulder GirdleElbow & WristWaist & PelvisKua & KneeFoot & AnkleThe Waist Program

Structure

In standing and walking, body weight rests on a surface less than one sixth of body height long. In Tai Chi we regularly put all of it on one foot — which sounds unstable, but if the body is aligned with gravity, gravity itself does the supporting.

The foot has three arches — medial longitudinal, lateral longitudinal, transverse. They are a spring system, not something to claw with.

The ideal base is a triangle: medial heel – head of the first metatarsal – head of the fifth metatarsal. All three touch, weight shared.

Principle

其根在腳，發於腿，主宰於腰，形於手指 — root in the feet, issue in the legs, governed by the waist, shaped in the fingers. It appears in Fu Zhongwen, Chen Xin, the Sun-style text and the Wu-style translation.

“Its root is in the feet, it issues from the legs, it is governed by the waist, and it takes shape in the fingers; from the feet to the legs to the waist, there must always be one complete qi.” — Fu Zhongwen, Ten Essentials, item 7

湧泉 (Yǒngquán) — the point in the sole, one third forward — is a sensory reference: the “bubbling spring” where force rises from the ground. It is a receiving point, not a pressing point.

Common Faults

FaultSignCorrection
Heel liftsHeel leaves the floor on turning or when tiredReduce turn range; check region 3 (a thrust chest lifts the heels)
Weight on the toesToes pale, sense of sliding forwardBring weight to mid-foot
Collapsed archMedial arch touches down, knee rolls inLift with muscle, not with toes
Clawing toesToes curled, tendons stand up on the instepLet the toes lie long, touching lightly
Over-turned footToes out more than 30°Bring back to 15–20° in the bow stance

Drills

DrillHowDosage

2. Toe yogaLift the big toe alone, then the others alone, alternating. Slow, no clawing.5 each pattern
3. Ankle mobilitySeated, circle the toes 10 times each way; flex-extend the ankle 15 times.1 min per side
4. Heel-to-toe shiftStanding, move weight from heel to toes and back, slowly, without lifting.10 reps

Cues & Gate

Cue: “Root at the heel, force at mid-foot.” When turning: “Heel stays, hips go first.”

Region 8 gate

  • Ten weight shifts: heels down, toes not clawing;
  • Single-leg 10 s each side, eyes open;
  • 45° turn with the foot fixed: no sense of slipping or knee torsion.

Pass → series complete. Return to region 6 with the 12-week program.
