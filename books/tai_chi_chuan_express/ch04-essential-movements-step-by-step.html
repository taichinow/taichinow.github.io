Chương 4: Essential Movements Step-by-Step

Tai Chi Chuan Express: Instant Stress Relief & Internal Energy — Vince Chee

Six distilled postures can be mastered in days: Serene Commencing; Parting the Wild Horse's Mane for thoracic expansion; and White Crane Spreads Wings for unilateral balance.

Brush Knee and Push synchronizes hip torque with forward thrust, while Wave Hands like Clouds offers unparalleled therapeutic relief for cervical stiffness and blood pressure regulation.

Performing each movement 3 to 5 times per side creates a moving meditation accessible in any modest office or living room space.
