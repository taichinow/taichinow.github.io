Chương 2: Basic Technical Essentials and Body Alignment

Taiji Quan 48 Forms (Chinese Wushu Series) — Chinese Wushu Series · Men Hui Feng

Footwork within the 48 Forms features dynamic diversity, incorporating Bow Stances, Empty Stances, Flat/Crouching Stances (Pu Bu), Cross-steps, and Horse Stances. Each step must be placed with cat-like quietness and mountain-like solidity.

Hand techniques encompass Palms, Fists, Hooks, and Finger strikes. The rhythmic opening and closing of the extremities corresponds directly with the internal pulsation of the Dantian.

The eyes must precede the movement of hands and weapons, radiating quiet focus, commanding presence, and seamless spatial awareness.
