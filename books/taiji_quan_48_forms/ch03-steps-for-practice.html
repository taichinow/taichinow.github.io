Chương 3: Steps for Practice & Methodical Progression

Taiji Quan 48 Forms (Chinese Wushu Series) — Chinese Wushu Series · Men Hui Feng

The primary stage focuses strictly on geometrical and structural precision. Practitioners isolate movements, ensuring proper angles, joint alignments, and balance before linking postures.

The intermediate phase emphasizes fluid continuity: 'Even if the physical motion pauses, the intent continues; if the intent pauses, the spiritual presence bridges the gap.' Movements must connect like pearls on a string.

The ultimate stage achieves spontaneous mind-body unity, moving effortlessly with the effortless grace of water while retaining immense latent martial strength.
