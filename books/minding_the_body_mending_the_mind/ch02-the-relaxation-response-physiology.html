Chương 2: The Relaxation Response: The Physiology of Meditation

Minding the Body, Mending the Mind — Joan Borysenko, Ph.D.

To counterbalance chronic sympathetic arousal, evolutionary biology endowed humans with an intrinsic self-healing antidote: The Relaxation Response.

Engaging in focused meditation or tranquil mantra repetition activates the vagus nerve brake, dropping heart rate, easing arterial tension, and diverting vital circulation back to digestion and cellular repair.

Electroencephalographic (EEG) recordings demonstrate rapid deceleration from agitated Beta frequencies to expansive Alpha and Theta rhythms, fostering optimal genetic repair and tissue recovery.
