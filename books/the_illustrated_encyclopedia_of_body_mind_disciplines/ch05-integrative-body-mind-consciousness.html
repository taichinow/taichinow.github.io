Chương 5: Integrative Mind-Body Consciousness and Embodiment

The Illustrated Encyclopedia of Body-Mind Disciplines — Nancy Allison, CMA

Modern civilization's tragic malaise is disembodiment—treating the somatic vessel merely as a mechanical vehicle transporting an overstimulated head between conference rooms.

This encyclopedia declares that the frontier of human flourishing lies in full embodiment—inhabiting every muscle fiber and respiratory cycle with lucent, appreciative presence.

Honoring bodily intelligence transforms the physical vessel into an enlightened sanctuary, unlocking innate wisdom, radiant vitality, and authentic peace.
