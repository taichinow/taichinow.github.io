Chương 4: Touch, Myofascial Release, and Neuromuscular Therapies

The Illustrated Encyclopedia of Body-Mind Disciplines — Nancy Allison, CMA

The human hand represents an unparalleled instrument of therapeutic diagnosis and healing. The Trager Approach introduces wave-like rocking motions, asking the central nervous system: 'How light and free can this body feel?'

Myofascial Trigger Point protocols decompress ischemic hyperirritable muscular nodules, restoring sarcomere length and extinguishing distant referred pain patterns.

Neuromuscular Therapy (NMT) re-establishes neuro-muscular homeostasis, systematically resolving chronic mechanical pain syndromes.
