Chương 2: What is the Empty Force? The Reality of Ling Kong Jing

Empty Force: The Ultimate Martial Art — The Power of Chi for Self Defense and Energy Healing — Paul Dong & Richard Raffill

Ling Kong Jing (Empty Force) stands as the pinnacle of esoteric internal arts, enabling an adept to repel, freeze, or destabilize opponents across space without physical contact.

Rather than sleight of hand or mere psychological suggestion, the phenomenon involves acute energetic resonance between the projector's bio-magnetic field and the recipient's nervous system.

Issuing focused intent through the Laogong palm acupoints induces sudden perturbations in the opponent's vestibular balance centers, precipitating abrupt somatic disequilibrium.
