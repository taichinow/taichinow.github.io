Chương 5: Injury Prevention and Biomechanical Conditioning

Biomechanical Principles of Tennis Technique: Using Science to Improve Your Strokes — Duane Knudson, Ph.D.

Athletic injuries predominantly stem from kinetic dysfunction and muscular imbalances between agonist drivers and stabilizing antagonists. Strengthening the posterior rotator cuff is mandatory to absorb repetitive deceleration loads.

Integrating Tai Chi loosening exercises helps athletes release chronic muscular holding patterns, restoring myofascial hydration and significantly prolonging professional competitive longevity.

The synthesis of Western biomechanical precision and Eastern internal somatic wisdom establishes an advanced paradigm for holistic athletic excellence.
