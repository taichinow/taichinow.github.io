Chương 2: Biomechanics of the Serve and Overhead

Biomechanical Principles of Tennis Technique: Using Science to Improve Your Strokes — Duane Knudson, Ph.D.

The tennis serve represents the most complex multi-segment motor action in athletics, demanding seamless coordination across an open kinetic chain.

During the trophy posture, deep knee flexion combined with thoracic tilt pre-stretches the pectoral and shoulder musculature, maximizing stored elastic potential energy via the stretch-shortening cycle.

At uncoiling, rapid internal shoulder rotation and forearm pronation exceed 1,500 degrees per second, propelling the ball at speeds exceeding 120 mph with pinpoint rotational control.
