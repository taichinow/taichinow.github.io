Chương 3: Clinical Nutritional Medicine, Micronutrients & Megavitamins

Healing with Complementary & Alternative Therapies — C. Norman Shealy, M.D., Ph.D.

Over 80% of urban populations suffer profound subclinical magnesium depletion due to industrialized soil exhaustion and refined food processing, causing spasticity, cardiac arrhythmias, and insomnia.

Dr. Shealy advocates transdermal magnesium chloride therapy paired with optimized vitamin C and D3/K2 supplementation to restore cellular membrane potentials.

Repairing the gut microbiome through fermented foods and targeted probiotics optimizes enteric neurotransmitter synthesis—generating over 90% of bodily serotonin to lift depressive mood.
