Chương 2: Acupuncture and Clinical Energy Meridian Networks

Healing with Complementary & Alternative Therapies — C. Norman Shealy, M.D., Ph.D.

Acupuncture stands as the most internationally validated complementary modality recognized by the World Health Organization (WHO) and NIH for refractory chronic pain.

Acupoint insertion excites myelinated A-delta fibers, engaging the spinal Gate Control mechanism to block slow C-fiber nociceptive transmission to higher cerebral centers.

Simultaneously, stimulation prompts the hypothalamic-pituitary axis to release massive cascades of beta-endorphins—endogenous opioids two hundred times more potent than morphine, devoid of addiction liabilities.
