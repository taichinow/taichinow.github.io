Chương 1: The Crisis of Modern Medicine and the Holistic Paradigm

Healing with Complementary & Alternative Therapies — C. Norman Shealy, M.D., Ph.D.

Dr. C. Norman Shealy, renowned neurosurgeon and founding president of the American Holistic Medical Association (AHMA), opens with a stark critique of pharmaceutical over-prescribing, opioid addiction, and unnecessary invasive surgeries.

Complementary and Alternative Medicine (CAM) does not reject emergency acute interventions, but provides safe, non-invasive protocols addressing root etiologies of chronic suffering.

Genuine health demands vibrant harmony across the complete human triad: physical soundness, mental serenity, and profound spiritual meaning.
