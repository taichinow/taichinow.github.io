Chương 1: Eastern Healing Traditions: Traditional Chinese Medicine & Ayurveda

The Healing Therapies Bible: Discover 70 Therapies — Claire Gillman

Claire Gillman curates an encyclopedic compendium documenting over 70 natural healing therapies from diverse civilizations, anchored by Eastern twin titans: Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Indian Ayurveda.

Both ancient disciplines perceive the human organism not as an isolated mechanical engine, but as an energetic continuum in dynamic communion with cosmic elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether). Healing restores balance rather than merely suppressing symptoms.

Acupoint stimulation and botanical pharmacology catalyze endogenous Chi and Prana circulation, awakening innate homeostasis.
