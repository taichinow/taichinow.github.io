Chương 5: Living in Accord with the Dao: Harmonizing the Spirit

Health and Long Life the Chinese Way — Livia Kohn, Ph.D.

The pinnacle of Chinese health cultivation resides in natural spontaneous simplicity (Wu-Wei)—acting without forced contrivance, flowing effortlessly with the Tao like water seeking the lowest ground.

Each season demands bio-rhythmic realignment: spring fosters creative emergence; summer expands outward vigor; autumn gathers inwardly; winter stores vital heat through early slumber and delayed rising.

When the spirit ceases to be tormented by avarice, anxiety, and resentment, Shen rests peacefully within the heart center, enabling humanity to fulfill its natural lifespan.
