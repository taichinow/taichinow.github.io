Chương 2: Daoyin Gymnastics and Meridian Stretching

Health and Long Life the Chinese Way — Livia Kohn, Ph.D.

Daoyin translates as 'guiding the Qi to achieve harmony, stretching the body to attain suppleness'—the historical fountainhead of Asian somatic movement therapy.

Mimicking the serpentine mobility of cranes, dragons, bears, and apes, Daoyin systematically elongates myofascial trains corresponding to acupuncture meridians.

Daily commitment preserves intervertebral disc hydration, eliminates spinal stiffness, and deters osteoarthritic joint degeneration.
