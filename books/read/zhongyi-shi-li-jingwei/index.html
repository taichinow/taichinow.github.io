中医史

🇨🇳 Chinese · PDF Scan · 520 pages (2 volumes)

by 李经纬伟 (Li Jingwei) · Hainan Press

About this book

Li Jingwei (b. 1929) founded the medical-history literature research office of the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences, served as director and honorary director of the China Medical History Museum, and was editor-in-chief of the General History of Chinese Medicine. This single-volume history runs from antiquity to the present: origins, the classic texts, schools of thought, materia medica, acupuncture, public health, and the long encounter with Western medicine. One of the standard Chinese-language histories of the field, written by the man who built much of its modern infrastructure.
