医者意也 · 认识中医

🇨🇳 Chinese · PDF Scan · 252 pages

by 廖育群 (Liao Yuqun) · Guangxi Normal University Press

About this book

Liao Yuqun — former director of the Institute for the History of Natural Science at the Chinese Academy of Sciences — takes the old phrase 医者意也 (“the physician’s mind”) as the key to understanding traditional Chinese medicine. Tracing the idea from the Book of the Later Han through the theory and practice of diagnosis and treatment, he asks what role intuition, judgment and imagination actually play in the physician’s art, and what that reveals about Chinese medicine as a system of thought. A short, philosophical entry point from the same author as the medical volume of the History of Science and Technology in China.

Tiếng Việt: đã dịch trọn Phần Một (chín tiểu luận) và Phần Hai (bốn tiểu luận, trang 1–61) — đọc bản dịch ↗. Các phần còn lại đang được thực hiện. Xem mục Đông y trong thư viện ↗
