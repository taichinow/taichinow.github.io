Chương 4: Applying Your Ki in Daily Life and Human Relations

The Book of Ki: A Practical Guide to the Healing Principles of Life Energy — Koichi Tohei (Đại sư Sáng lập Ki Society)

Ki training finds its highest expression not on the dojo mat, but within everyday reality: driving in traffic, executive decision-making, and navigating high-stakes interpersonal negotiations.

Anchoring awareness at the One Point shields the mind against anxiety and reactivity, fostering clear perception akin to a placid lake mirroring the heavens without ripples.

Confronted with conflict, the practitioner yields without weakness, harmonizing with opposing momentum to dissolve confrontation into constructive synergy.
