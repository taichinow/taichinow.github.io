Chương 2: Accessing and Sensing Your Ki

The Book of Ki: A Practical Guide to the Healing Principles of Life Energy — Koichi Tohei (Đại sư Sáng lập Ki Society)

Chronic somatic tension blocks the natural pulsation of life energy. Cultivating Ki begins by distinguishing between passive limpness and positive living relaxation.

In the classic Unbendable Arm exercise, tensing the biceps creates rigidity that easily buckles under pressure. Conversely, relaxing fully while visualizing a torrent of water surging effortlessly through the fingertips renders the arm unyielding without strain.

Mind leads body: wherever conscious intention travels, bio-energy streams instantaneously, awakening the latent reserves of human neurophysiology.
