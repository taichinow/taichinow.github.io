Chương 6: How to Practise Taiji Quan

Basics of Taiji Quan — Li Xingdong · Foreign Languages Press Beijing

The central canon instructs: 'Use intent, not brute physical force.' When the mind achieves profound calmness, alpha brainwaves activate parasympathetic tone, dilating capillaries and optimizing microcirculation.

Breathing must remain continuous, abdominal, and harmonious: inhaling during lifting and gathering movements, exhaling during sinking and issuing movements.

Practitioners must diligently avoid common biomechanical errors such as protruding the knee past the toe box, shrugging the shoulders, rounding the lower back, or holding the breath.
