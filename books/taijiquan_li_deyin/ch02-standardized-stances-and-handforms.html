Chương 2: Standardized Stances, Hand Configurations, and Body Alignments

Taijiquan: Complete Guide by Master Li Deyin — Li Deyin (Lý Đức Ấn)

In international championship arenas, structural precision determines technical marks. The Bow Stance requires the forward femur parallel to the floor, the rear leg extended with foot at 45 degrees, and a lateral width equal to shoulder width.

Palms require 'sitting wrists' (Zuo Wan), with fingers gently curved like willow leaves, channeling kinetic force cleanly through the heel or outer knife-edge of the hand.

During unilateral balances (Du Li Bu), the standing foot roots deeply, the elevated knee rises above hip height, and the spine maintains unflinching vertical alignment.
