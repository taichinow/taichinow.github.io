Chương 1: Stepping onto the Path: Beginning Chen Style Taiji

Turning Silk: A Diary of Chen Taiji Practice — Kartha Cochran

Kartha Cochran offers an intimate, lyrical, and unflinchingly candid diary capturing years of rigorous Chen style Taijiquan apprenticeship under Grandmaster Chen Xiaowang.

Dispelling stereotypes of passive park calisthenics, traditional Chen training confronts the novice with deep crouching stances, exhausting leg fatigue, and relentless Zhan Zhuang demands.

The memoir chronicles dismantling Western intellectual arrogance, surrendering to somatic exhaustion, and learning to listen to the organic intelligence of bodily wisdom.
