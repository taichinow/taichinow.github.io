Wu Mengxia (1958) · 101 pages translated · 245 figures

Taijiquan: Nine Keys & Eighty-One Forms — Full English Translation

Wu Mengxia and Wu Zhaofeng's 1958 People's Sports Publishing House handbook, from the Yang Banhou lineage — the form the book itself calls the "Great Frame" (大功架), close to Yang Chengfu's large frame. Translated page by page with the Chinese source kept alongside: the twenty-eight line 全体大用诀 and the other eight keys with their line-by-line annotation, the five essential maxims, and the complete eighty-one-posture method from the Wuji Preparatory Posture to Closing Taiji — each posture carrying its own combat-meaning section. All 245 posture photographs are cropped from the scan and placed where the printed page put them; the "repeated form" postures that only cross-reference earlier figures correctly carry none.
