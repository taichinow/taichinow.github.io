Chương 4: The Diverse Clinical Manifestations of TMS

The Mindbody Prescription: Healing the Body, Healing the Pain — John E. Sarno, M.D.

TMS is an ingenious shape-shifter. Whenever a patient stops fearing pain in one anatomical site, the brain smoothly transfers symptoms elsewhere: shifting from lumbar sciatica to cervical rigidity, or expressing as irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), gastroesophageal reflux, or chronic migraines.

Countless thousands diagnosed with repetitive strain injury (RSI) or carpal tunnel syndrome suffer not from keyboard biomechanics, but from workplace anxiety and suppressed perfectionist resentment.

Pervasive diagnoses such as fibromyalgia represent generalized, systemic variations of this identical emotion-driven ischemic mechanism.
