中国医学史 · 第2版

🇨🇳 Chinese · PDF Scan · 596 pages (2 volumes)

edited by 甄志亚 (Zhen Zhiya) · People’s Medical Publishing House, 2nd ed. 2008

About this book

The standard teaching reference for colleges of traditional Chinese medicine across China, in its second edition (2008). The book walks the full sweep of Chinese medical history — primitive medicine, the Qin–Han classics, the medieval period of synthesis, the Song–Jin–Yuan schools, the Ming–Qing consolidation, and the modern era of integration with Western medicine — with chapter summaries and review questions. If you want one systematic Chinese-language overview of how the medicine was built century by century, this is the textbook the whole field was trained on.
