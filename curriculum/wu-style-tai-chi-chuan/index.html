Wu Style Tai Chi Chuan — Complete Course of Study

A professional, sequenced curriculum built on the official Course of Study of the International Wu Style Tai Chi Chuan Federation, with every module mapped to the books, technique notes, video library and research articles available on TaichiNOW.

Source: International Wu Style Tai Chi Chuan Federation · wustyle.com/learning/course-of-study · Copyright © 2017 International Wu Style Tai Chi Chuan Federation

How this curriculum is built

The Wu Style Course of Study is organised in three graded levels — Beginners, Intermediate and Advanced — followed by a Weapons Class and an Instructor Training Class. The Federation publishes the syllabus as a single overview image; the exact module names, sequences and form lists reproduced below are taken from that official syllabus and from the Federation's companion Forms & Techniques page.

What this page adds is the study layer. Every module below is matched with the specific TaichiNOW resources that teach it — including full Vietnamese translations where they exist — so a student or an instructor can follow the Federation's syllabus end to end without hunting for material. Timing, prerequisites and assessment criteria are TaichiNOW's own recommendations and are marked as such.

Level 1 — Beginners

4–12 monthsPrerequisite: none108 movements

Learning objectives

  • Perform the complete 108-movement Wu style hand form with correct frame, weight transfer and coordinated breathing.
  • Apply the basic principles — relaxation, upright posture, dantian-led movement, empty/solid distinction — throughout the form.
  • Execute the five warm-up exercises correctly and independently before every session.
  • Walk forward and backward in the Tai Chi manner without breaking the frame.

Official syllabus (International Wu Style Tai Chi Chuan Federation)

  • Wu's T'ai Chi Chuan — the full set of 108 forms
  • Tai Chi Chuan Basic Principles
  • 5 Warm-up Exercises
  • Basic Tai Chi Walking (forward & backward)

Assessment

  • Demonstrate the five warm-up exercises unaided.
  • Walk forward and backward across the room maintaining frame and empty/solid distinction.
  • Perform the 108 form from memory, with the instructor permitted two prompts only.
  • State and demonstrate the basic principles in response to questions.

Level 2 — Intermediate

12–24 monthsPrerequisite: Level 1

Learning objectives

  • Perform basic push hands with adherence, listening energy and the four primary energies.
  • Execute the 9-Palace and circular 9-Palace stepping patterns with a partner.
  • Fall safely — break-fall technique without bracing.
  • Generate power (fa jing) with loose fist, elbow and An.
  • Demonstrate the named applications against a cooperative partner.
  • Refine the form: remove residual tension, smooth transitions, deepen the frame.

Official syllabus

  • Basic Push Hands
  • 9-Palace, Circular 9-Palace
  • Break-fall training
  • Power Generation (Fa jing): Loose Fist, Elbow, An
  • Application: Grasp Bird's Tail; Needle at Season Bottom; White Crane Spread Wing; Cloud Hand; Single Lotus; Double Lotus
  • Form Refinement

Assessment

  • Basic push hands for three minutes without arm-wrestling, maintaining adherence and Ting Jin.
  • Demonstrate both 9-Palace patterns with a partner.
  • Break-fall from a standing push in both directions.
  • Four fa jing releases (loose fist, elbow, An, plus one of the student's choice) at full intent without loss of structure.
  • All six named applications demonstrated and explained.

Level 3 — Advanced

24–60 monthsPrerequisite: Level 2

Learning objectives

  • Free-style push hands with multiple partners and escalating resistance.
  • Roll-elbow, circular and Ta-Lu push hands — the four advanced push-hands formats.
  • The eight elements of power generation, integrated rather than isolated.
  • The four methods of application, applied under time pressure.
  • Advanced applications: combining, chaining and selecting techniques.

Official syllabus

  • Free-style Push Hands
  • Roll-Elbow Push Hands
  • Circular Push Hands
  • Ta-Lu Push Hands
  • Eight Elements of Power Generation
  • Four Methods of Application
  • Advanced applications
  • Form Refinement

  • Free-style push hands, rotating partners, five minutes each, with honest resistance.

Weapons Class

12 months per weaponPrerequisite: Level 2

Official syllabus

  • Tai Chi Broad Sword (dao)
  • Tai Chi Sword (jian)
  • Tai Chi Spear (qiang)

All three weapons extend the hand form: the weapon is a longer lever on the same silk-reeling body. Take them in the order listed — broad sword first, because it is closest to the empty-hand mechanics, then sword, then spear.
